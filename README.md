### How I work

- **Start from the problem and the metric**, not the feature.
- **Prototype to learn.** A working prototype in front of a customer gets sharper feedback than any spec.
- **Measure AI before shipping it.** Golden sets and launch gates, not vibes.
- **Verify, don't assume.** "Done" means built, deployed and checked, whether a person or an agent says it.
